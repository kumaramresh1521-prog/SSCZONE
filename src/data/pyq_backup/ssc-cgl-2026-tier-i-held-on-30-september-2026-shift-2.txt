Q15. Choose the correct option to complete the series. Citrus : Lemon :: Rosaceae: ? श्रृंखला को पूरा करने के लिए सही विकल्प चुनें। साइट्रस : Lemon :: Rosaceae: ?
A) Hibiscus
B) Mango
C) Pear
D) Cleopatra
Correct Answer: Option C
Explanation: The relationship in the first pair is Family : Example (Plant/Fruit).
• Citrus : Lemon
	• Citrus is a plant family (specifically the genus/group of rue family Rutaceae often referred to broadly as citrus). Lemon belongs to this category.
• Rosaceae : ?
	• Rosaceae is the rose family of flowering plants.
	• Among the given options, Pear belongs to the Rosaceae family (which also includes apples, peaches, plums, and strawberries).

Why the other options are incorrect:

• A Hibiscus — Belongs to the Malvaceae family (mallow family).
• B Mango — Belongs to the Anacardiaceae family (cashew or sumac family).
• D Cleopatra — This is not a plant family or a standard biological classification related to Rosaceae in this context (it is a variety of mandarin orange, which would fall under Citrus).
